Food and drink, since 2017

Where to eat and drink in KL.

In 2017, TEG Media began EatDrink.my in partnership with the team behind the well-known Eat Drink KL food blog, creating a dedicated platform for exploring Malaysia’s vibrant dining and lifestyle scene. Over time, the editorial directions of the two entities have evolved independently, but TEG Media continues to operate and maintain the EatDrink.my website, as well as its affiliated EatDrink section on ExpatGo.com.

Today, EatDrink.my provides an engaging mix of restaurant reviews, dining features, food and beverage stories, and coverage of lifestyle events, with a particular focus on Greater Kuala Lumpur and occasionally destinations elsewhere in Malaysia. The site covers everything from new restaurant openings and established favourites to wine, whisky, cocktails, food trends, culinary personalities, and special dining experiences.

With a notable archive and an ongoing programme of original content, EatDrink.my offers readers a trusted source of ideas and insights for discovering where to eat, drink, and enjoy the region. Its audience includes both Malaysian diners and international residents looking to explore Kuala Lumpur’s remarkably diverse food and lifestyle landscape.
