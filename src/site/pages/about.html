Who we are

Started with one magazine. Built a group.

Starting with The Expat magazine in 1996, TEG Media (The Expat Group) has grown into a media group providing information about Malaysia to English-speaking audiences.

We reach them through our publications, ExpatGo, our newsletters, our events and our visa services. We aim to show Malaysia at its best, and we build marketing campaigns for the businesses and organisations that want to reach our readers.

Andy DavisonCEO and Founder

Andy founded The Expat in 1996 and still leads the group. He speaks on the MM2H programme in Malaysia and overseas.

Our story
