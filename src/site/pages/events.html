About WhiskyPLUS

In 2018, TEG Media successfully launched the inaugural edition of what has since become Malaysia’s largest and longest-running whisky and spirits tasting event, WhiskyPLUS. Created to bring whisky enthusiasts, curious newcomers, industry professionals, and leading brands together under one roof, WhiskyPLUS offers guests the opportunity to discover, taste, and learn about an exceptional range of whiskies from around the world.

Held in Kuala Lumpur, the event has grown significantly since its launch, with hundreds of guests attending each edition and more than 120 brands represented at recent events. Alongside an extensive selection of Scotch, Japanese, Irish, American, and other international whiskies, WhiskyPLUS also showcases selected boutique spirits, including gin, rum, vodka, and more, together with fine cigars, masterclasses, specialty foods, and other complementary premium lifestyle experiences. Designed as both an entertaining social occasion and an informative tasting experience, WhiskyPLUS welcomes everyone from seasoned connoisseurs to those taking their first steps into the world of whisky.

Over the years, WhiskyPLUS has established itself as a major fixture on Malaysia’s whisky calendar, expanding beyond Kuala Lumpur with a Penang edition and continuing to build strong relationships with whisky and spirits principals, importers, distributors, retailers, and event partners. For more information, visit www.whiskyplus.my.
