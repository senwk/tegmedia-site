Our agency

From first question to approval.

The government requires MM2H applications to go through a licensed agent. Our agency, Borneo Vision (MM2H), has been an approved agent since 2005 and takes applicants from their first enquiry through to approval.

We focus on English-speaking applicants, and for the principal application we settle the government charges ourselves.

JohorSEZ and SFZ

The lower-cost version in the special economic and financial zones.
